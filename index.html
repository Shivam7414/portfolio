01 / 07 · 2026 · Personal finance app

FinCard

A report card for your money, with a coach attached.

01 · The problem

Am I doing okay with money?

Most people know their salary and roughly what their EMI is. Ask whether they're doing okay overall, though, and there's no real answer. The pieces are spread across bank apps, a forgotten SIP and some gold at home.

02 · The score

One score out of 100

You answer a few questions about what you earn, owe, spend and own. FinCard turns that into a balance sheet and a health score, and the score is always free.

The score has two halves, monthly habits and what you've built, so owning a big house can't hide bad habits. I tested the maths against 200,000 generated profiles.

03 · The checks

The weakest spot shows up first

Eight checks explain where the score came from, with the one that needs the most work at the top. Each check shows the actual sum in your own rupees, so nothing feels like a black box.

Checks you haven't revealed yet stay on the server, so there's nothing hidden inside the app for anyone to dig out.

04 · The simulator

Try a change before you make it

Wondering what clearing your credit card would do? Drag a slider and watch the score update straight away, before any real money moves.

Credits go through a single locked transaction with an idempotency key, so tapping twice never charges twice.

02 / 07 · Maxaix · 2024 · Delivery, rides, shopping

SendMePls

Parcels, rides and shopping, with the customer and the rider watching the same order live.

01 · The problem

Everyone is watching the same order

The customer and the rider look at the same order from two different phones. If one screen falls even a few seconds behind, people start calling support.

02 · Real-time

Live location without the lag

I built the real-time core. Each location ping from the rider goes into Redis and is pushed out over WebSockets, so both screens move together.

03 · Busy hours

Orders that never get mixed up

Peak hours are where apps usually break. A queue and job layer keeps every order update in the right order, and a small AI step estimates prices for shopping requests.

05 / 07 · Maxaix · Internal · Procurement

        
RfqAI

        
An AI tool that turns tender documents into priced estimates.

      

      

        

          

            
01 · The problem

            
Days of reading and copying

            
Pricing a tender meant someone going through pages of line items by hand, looking up each rate and typing it into a spreadsheet. It took days.

          

          

            
02 · Extraction

            
Let the model do the reading

            
The document is split into chunks, and an LLM pulls out every line item with its quantity and unit, in the same order as the tender.

          

          

            
03 · Pricing

            
Prices pulled from past quotes

            
Each item is matched against a vector store of old quotes and current market rates. What used to take days now comes out as a finished report.
